Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy describes how the mobile app “Better Moments For Better Life” (the “App”) handles information.

1. Summary

The App works locally on your device. We do not run a server for the App, we do not create user accounts, and we do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

To work, the App stores the following only on your device: your favorite practices, the practices shown to you and their history, your settings (language and appearance), your reminder settings, and the status of your premium purchase. This data never leaves your device through the App. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it.

3. Notifications

If you allow it, the App schedules local notifications on your device. They are created and shown by your device and are not sent through our servers. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

4. Purchases

Premium access is purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Apple or Google; we do not see or store your payment details. The App receives only confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can unlock premium features. Apple and Google handle this information under their own privacy policies.

5. No analytics, advertising, or tracking

The App does not contain advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
